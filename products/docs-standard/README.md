# VSlices Docs Standard — product-owned conceptual source

**Semantic owner:** VSlices Docs Standard. **Hosting:** `the-alive-lab/vslices-suite`.

## Purpose

Preserve useful knowledge and continuity without requiring documentary ceremony. Documentation should protect meaningful connections among context, intention, decisions, realization, validation and evolution.

## Current documentary responsibilities

- **Support Note:** a detail about a specific concept.
- **Document:** a large question about a specific concept.
- **Nexus:** a collection of large questions about a specific concept.
- **Continuity Path:** a map of question-bearing routes preserving continuity around a specific topic.

The types' [root questions](root-question-inventory.md) are extracted from the normative [vslices/docs-standard](https://github.com/vslices/docs-standard). Branches emerge and stabilize progressively through observed Semantic Pressure, never from a requirement to complete an exhaustive tree. Continuity Paths have no intrinsic core/supporting/contextual or primary/secondary rank; select N paths according to real continuity needs.

## Authority and relations

The `vslices/docs-standard` repository remains the **normative, versioned, machine-consumable vocabulary currently used by Tooling**. This Suite namespace is being developed as the canonical home for product meaning, **without silently superseding that operational source**, its manifest promotion state or question-graph semantics. Questions of transition and synchronization require explicit decisions.

Docs Standard owns documentary semantics; Method owns Semantic Pressure and contextual coordination; Tooling realizes supported authoring and validation mechanisms; Design owns its own phases and modalities.

## Provenance

The non-artifact concepts were reconstructed from the historical public product surface [vslices/docs/es/products/docs-standard](https://github.com/vslices/docs/tree/main/es/products/docs-standard). Its *artifact-specific documentary definitions* are legacy and cannot override `vslices/docs-standard`; the original history remains unchanged.
