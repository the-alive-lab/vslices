# VSlices Method

**Conceptual owner:** VSlices Method.  
**Status:** approved core responsibility; specific operational criteria remain under review.

## Purpose and boundaries

Method **coordinates how the distinct products and mechanisms of VSlices participate in real work**, choosing how to navigate them in light of uncertainty, priorities, evidence and the continuity at risk. Its unit is **VSlices product responsibilities**, not necessarily multiple external customer projects. One software project may require several VSlices products.

Method does not thereby own the meanings of the products it coordinates.

- [Design](../design/README.md) owns problem-solving methodologies, the Context-/Problem-/Slice-First modality definitions and the five Design phases.
- **Method** owns contextual selection and switching of these Design modalities; it may use cross-Suite mechanisms such as [Semantic Pressure](../../mechanisms/semantic-pressure.md), whose base meaning is Method-owned even when others use it.
- [Protocols](../protocols/README.md) owns reusable operational procedures and execution continuity; it applies Method judgments rather than redefining them.
- [Docs Standard](../docs-standard/README.md) can preserve documentary relationships and knowledge without becoming the owner of Method's decisions.

These are complementary responsibilities, not a fixed hierarchy requiring every run to visit every VSlices product.

## Canonical Method sources

- [Orientation](orientation.md) — **approved Method-owned conceptual responsibility** to recover, contrast and update actors' and teams' situated continuity. Not a sixth Design phase; **formally recommended at process start and between every Design iteration when using VSlices Method**, without a compulsory gate. Detailed reconciliation mechanisms remain candidates.
- [Modality Selection](modality-selection.md) — choose which Design modality should guide the next responsible effort.
- [Modality Switching](modality-switching.md) — reconsider that choice when evidence, uncertainty, consequences or continuity change.
- [Context Guides](context-guides/README.md) — recognizable situations and bounded responses, including unfamiliar/digitalized work, joining existing work and introducing VSlices Method progressively.
- [Semantic Pressure](../../mechanisms/semantic-pressure.md) — methodological pressure to preserve material distinctions and continuity, with Suite-wide applicability.

The approved Design cycle is [Understanding → Contextualizing → Planning → Building → Validating → Understanding](../design/lifecycle.md). **Validating closes an iteration by producing retrospective artifacts and responses to feedback received (if any).** They feed the recommended Method Orientation before the next iteration, where continuation and modality can be reconsidered, without transferring Design phase authority into Method.

## Candidate priority interpretation

[Value Orientations](../../mechanisms/value-orientations.md) is the current provisional name for the three historical Advancement Paths, understood as **short-term business value / long-term business value / business value at least justified effort during a standalone iteration**. Method considers these alongside interpreted [Orientation Factors](../../forces/README.md), uncertainty (local/general), organizational needs and operating context when selecting or reconsidering a Design modality. The relationships and extreme-case affinities remain candidate, not a validated automatic choice mechanism. See [Modality Selection](modality-selection.md).

## History and evolution

The [first boundary review](../design-method-boundary-review.md) and [counterexample assessment](../design-method-boundary-assessment.md) preserve how earlier distinctions and misunderstandings were examined. Those records include historical open questions that have since been conceptually resolved; prefer the current sources linked above for present meaning.

The [public Method documentation](https://github.com/vslices/docs/tree/main/es/products/method) is a didactic/historical projection. New operational procedures belong to Protocols and product-specific realizations remain in their official specialized sources.

Method's further evolution should come from specific cases demonstrating missing coordination or continuity, rather than an exhaustive central orchestration scheme.
