# Context Guides — VSlices Method

**Owner:** VSlices Method. **Status:** canonical migration of existing context guidance, 2026-10-09.

Context Guides support applying Method in differing **real-work situations**. They help identify where the team is entering, which uncertainty threatens the next responsible decision, which Design modality might be appropriate, and which continuity deserves preservation. They do **not** require an adoption sequence, all available Continuity Paths, exhaustive documentation or a particular architecture.

## Existing guides

- [Non-digitalized work](non-digitalized.md): work exists primarily in people's practices, documents and manual coordination, before software represents it.
- [Digitalized work](digitalized.md): software represents some work but is evidence of domain behavior, not automatically domain truth.
- [Joining existing work](joining-existing-project.md): an actor joins ongoing work and needs enough historical/active continuity to contribute without resetting it.
- [Introducing Method](introducing-method.md): introduce a small useful continuity seam within current work rather than imposing a wholesale process.

These **situations may overlap** and apply to an entire project or smaller bounded work. They are not Design modalities. See Method's [Modality Selection](../modality-selection.md) and [Modality Switching](../modality-switching.md).

## Responsibility distinctions

- **Method:** chooses how participating VSlices responsibilities should enter and coordinate in the present context, and may select/switch modalities in response to uncertainty, priorities and continuity.
- **Design:** defines Context-/Problem-/Slice-First and the five phases (including Validating).
- **Docs Standard:** defines documents and Continuity Paths; a guide may suggest using a path but cannot redefine its semantics or mandate each path.
- **Planifications:** may enact a guide during a specific run, without becoming authority for its meaning.

A **Context Guide is not Orientation**. Its use can help an incoming actor orient, but whether Orientation as a general concept belongs to Method or Design is still unresolved. Do not infer ownership from naming, onboarding examples or document location.

## Migration notes

Source: [Method Context Guides](https://github.com/vslices/docs/blob/main/es/products/method/context-guides/index.md), with the four scenario pages linked from there.

Retained: context-dependent usefulness, minimum sufficient continuity, modality fit as **candidate** rather than command, avoidance of ceremony, and preserving only knowledge future work would otherwise rediscover.

The published guides contain detailed optional affinity tables and examples of Docs Standard documents. They remain evidence for subsequent evaluation of their concepts, **not universally binding taxonomies** in this first migration.

**Discrepancy retained for review:** the public *Introducing Method* guide suggests Slice-First in one case where the surrounding context is “too uncertain to intervene safely”. That condition alone does not justify Slice-First under Design's definition; a bounded slice still requires enough context and safety. Do not promote that row as a selection rule. The guide records this conflict without altering historical text.

Do not treat feedback as a separate mandatory Design stage: **Validating** may incorporate and respond to feedback **if received**, and its evidence may inform later Method decisions.
