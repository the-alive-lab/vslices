# Joining existing work — Context Guide

**Owner:** VSlices Method. **Status:** migrated context guidance.

## Situation and principal risk

A person or team enters work already in progress, with existing decisions, intent, artifacts, behavior, stakeholders and feedback. VSlices adoption may or may not already exist.

**Risk:** executing tickets or modifying implementation before understanding the continuity and consequences that made the work necessary; the opposite error is requiring complete historical reconstruction before any contribution.

## Method guidance

Join the **continuity of active work before the task list**. Identify the smallest relevant work item and recover its intent, current state, decisions, constraints, affected responsibilities and unresolved uncertainty. Distinguish inherited knowledge worth preserving from outdated or accidental commitments. Contribute to a bounded piece; preserve discovered gaps useful to the next participant.

Use Method's selection criteria:
- Context-First if surrounding context is the obstacle.
- Problem-First if a specific unexplained problem is the obstacle.
- Slice-First if a small safe contribution will clarify the work.

This is a context guide about **incorporation**, not proof that Orientation must be an independent Design phase. The general concept of Orientation and the authority for refresh remain under review.

Docs Standard may preserve vocabulary, decisions, behavioral intent, process and validation findings if they affect future work. No exhaustive onboarding packet is required.
