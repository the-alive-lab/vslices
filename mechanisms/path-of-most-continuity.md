# Path of Most Continuity

## Status

`candidate`

## Ownership

`VSlices Suite`

## Purpose

**Path of Most Continuity** is a VSlices mechanism for favoring advances that best preserve the ability to continue work without unnecessary reconstruction of its history, meaning, evidence, decisions, or unresolved state.

Its purpose is not to maximize documentation.

Documentation is one possible realization of continuity preservation, but continuity may also be preserved through lineage, relationships, explicit uncertainty, orientation, decision rationale, executable evidence, or other reconstructible traces.

## Current tendency

Path of Most Continuity tends to preserve what a future continuation would otherwise have to rediscover.

Possible movements include:

- recording why a decision was made;
- preserving relevant evidence;
- maintaining lineage between transformations;
- making uncertainty explicit;
- updating orientation after meaningful change;
- relating artifacts that would otherwise become disconnected;
- preserving rejected alternatives when their rejection matters later;
- keeping implementation and intent traceably connected.

## Typical questions

- What would a future continuation have to reconstruct if we stop now?
- Which knowledge is currently present only in a person's or session's working context?
- What history or rationale will disappear if it is not preserved?
- Which relationship between artifacts is currently implicit?
- What uncertainty must remain visible rather than silently resolved?
- What is the smallest continuity artifact that prevents expensive reconstruction later?

## Continuity is not accumulation

More preserved material does not automatically mean more continuity.

A large document can reduce continuity if it obscures what matters, duplicates authority, or becomes expensive to maintain.

Path of Most Continuity favors useful reconstructibility rather than maximal retention.

## Relationship with modalities

Path of Most Continuity may often favor Context-First when surrounding meaning, history, authority, or relationships are at risk of being lost.

This is a tendency, not an equivalence.

A small executable slice may provide more continuity when it preserves the relationship between an intention and observed behavior better than another document would.

## Limits

Path of Most Continuity does not determine advancement by itself.

Used alone, it can over-favor preservation, explanation, or documentary work even when the preserved material has low expected value or when a cheaper movement would retain enough continuity.

It must therefore remain distinguishable from Path of Least Resistance and Path of Most Value.
