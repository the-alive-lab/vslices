# Path of Most Value

## Status

`candidate`

## Ownership

`VSlices Suite`

## Purpose

**Path of Most Value** is a VSlices mechanism for favoring advances that are expected to contribute most to the current intention.

Value is not restricted to software delivery or business value.

Depending on the work, relevant value may include:

- resolving a user need;
- reducing a critical uncertainty;
- discovering semantic ownership;
- producing falsifying or discriminating evidence;
- reducing important technical complexity;
- enabling a blocked continuation;
- validating or rejecting a candidate model;
- improving safety, reliability, or comprehensibility.

## Current tendency

Path of Most Value tends to prioritize among valid movements according to the contribution they are expected to make now.

This may justify doing more expensive work when its expected contribution is materially greater.

## Typical questions

- What are we actually trying to achieve in this iteration?
- Which movement contributes most to that intention?
- Which uncertainty is most valuable to resolve?
- Which result would most change what we do next?
- Which action unlocks the most important continuation?
- Are we optimizing delivery value while ignoring epistemic, semantic, technical, or continuity value?

## Relationship with modalities

Path of Most Value may often favor Problem-First when a concrete pressure or need dominates the current intention.

This is a tendency, not an equivalence.

Context-First may provide the most value when misunderstanding the surrounding domain would invalidate later work.

Slice-First may provide the most value when executable feedback is the most informative next result.

## Limits

Path of Most Value does not determine advancement by itself.

Used alone, it can over-favor immediate contribution while accumulating unnecessary cost, destroying continuity, or repeatedly postponing work whose benefit is indirect but necessary.

It must therefore remain distinguishable from Path of Least Resistance and Path of Most Continuity.

## Open question

The current suite recognizes Least Resistance, Most Continuity, and Most Value as distinct advancement mechanisms.

It is not yet known whether these three are exhaustive, independent, or the correct fundamental factorization.

That question belongs to Research rather than being settled by this candidate definition.
