# Path of Least Resistance

## Status

`candidate`

## Ownership

`VSlices Suite`

## Purpose

**Path of Least Resistance** is a VSlices mechanism for favoring advances that require the least unnecessary effort, transformation, disruption, or commitment from the current state.

It does not mean "do the easiest thing".

Resistance is contextual. A locally easy action may create more total resistance if it requires unsupported assumptions, unnecessary restructuring, premature commitments, or avoidable work.

## Current tendency

Path of Least Resistance tends to preserve the current state unless a relevant pressure justifies movement.

A valid outcome may therefore be:

```text
current state
-> no change yet
```

when changing would introduce more cost or commitment than the available evidence justifies.

When movement is warranted, it favors the smallest defensible movement that responds to the current pressure.

## Typical questions

- What can remain unchanged?
- What is the smallest movement that responds to the current pressure?
- Which alternative requires fewer transformations?
- Which option introduces less accidental complexity?
- Which commitment can be delayed until evidence requires it?
- Are we doing work only because we assumed progress must mean change?

## Relationship with forces

Work forces influence what counts as resistance.

For example:

- resource forces may make time, money, infrastructure, or cognitive load dominant;
- technical forces may make coupling or migration cost dominant;
- delivery forces may make delayed feedback dominant;
- semantic forces may make unsupported assumptions themselves a source of resistance.

Path of Least Resistance does not replace those forces. It uses the current force profile to reason about the cost of possible movements.

## Relationship with modalities

Path of Least Resistance may often favor Slice-First because small executable movements can expose feedback cheaply.

This is a tendency, not an equivalence.

A Context-First or Problem-First movement may have lower resistance when implementation would otherwise require broad guessing, semantic reconstruction, or premature commitments.

## Limits

Path of Least Resistance does not determine advancement by itself.

Used alone, it can over-favor local preservation, incremental realization, or low-cost movement even when continuity or value would justify a more expensive action.

It must therefore remain distinguishable from other advancement mechanisms such as Path of Most Continuity and Path of Most Value.
