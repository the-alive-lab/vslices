# Semantic Pressure

## Status

`candidate`

## Ownership

`VSlices Suite`

## Purpose

**Semantic Pressure** is a VSlices mechanism for deliberately interrogating the meaning of a phenomenon until hidden distinctions, assumptions, constraints, authority, consequences, and required evidence become visible.

It does not exist to maximize questions or depth.

It should be applied until:

- relevant uncertainty has been reduced; or
- missing knowledge is made explicit and must be obtained rather than invented.

## Typical questions

- What exactly does this term mean?
- What distinguishes it from a similar concept?
- Who has authority to establish this fact?
- What must be true before or after?
- What evidence is needed?
- Where can that evidence come from?
- What can be derived and what must be observed?
- What can fail in an expected way?
- What changes as a consequence?

## Product relationships

### Design

May use Semantic Pressure to deepen understanding and challenge candidate models or boundaries.

### Method

May decide when, how far, and for what purpose to apply Semantic Pressure during real work.

### Docs Standard

May preserve distinctions, uncertainties, decisions, or evidence discovered through the mechanism.

### Framework

May consume the results when meaning, authority, evidence, or behavior must be represented as executable semantics.

### Tooling

May eventually assist the mechanism without gaining authority to decide what meaning is correct.

## Reconstructed evolution

Semantic Pressure was previously described as a candidate Method technique.

Current evidence suggests its identity crosses product boundaries, so it is provisionally reclassified as a suite-level mechanism.

The trajectory is intentionally preserved:

```text
candidate Method technique
-> observed cross-product use
-> ownership review
-> suite-level mechanism candidate
```

This avoids rewriting history as if the current classification had always been obvious.
