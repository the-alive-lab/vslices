# Reasoning reconstruction gap — from procedures to their originating judgments

**Status:** candidate cross-product investigation, **not** a newly established mechanism.  
**Origin:** observation clarified by Hernán on 2026-10-12, following the correction of [Research Input #23](https://github.com/vslices/research-space/issues/23).

## Observation

VSlices has increasingly explicit **procedures, modalities, artifacts, rules and product responsibility boundaries**, but some of the judgments by which its author **arrived at or selected them** were exercised intuitively rather than articulated as reconstructible criteria.

A good description of **what to do** or **what has been decided** does not automatically explain **why that alternative was selected rather than another**. The existence of a usable procedure does not prove that the originating reasoning has been modeled.

One known witness is [the advancement-path investigation](path-of-least-resistance.md): `Path of Least Resistance` alone did **not** adequately represent Hernán's reasoning for why an iteration should begin with Context-First, Problem-First or Slice-First depending on its situation. The later proposals `Path of Most Continuity` and `Path of Most Value` are **candidate explanatory distinctions**, not demonstrated sufficient causes or a completed optimizing theory.

The broader observation is that this **explanatory gap may recur in other parts of VSlices**. No inventory of those occurrences has yet been established, and no single common mechanism can currently be inferred.

## The distinction to preserve

```text
observed concrete situation and available evidence
  -> actual judgment / selection (sometimes tacit)
  -> procedure, concept or decision used
  -> observed result and subsequent revision
```

Reconstructing the middle movement is **not the same as** producing a plausible retrospective justification for the selected result.

Keep separate:

1. **Procedure:** the current reusable sequence or coordination of actions.
2. **Conceptual contract:** what the chosen concepts and boundaries mean.
3. **Decision rationale:** what the actor actually attended to, distinguished, preferred, avoided or treated as uncertain at the time.
4. **Candidate explanatory mechanism:** a revisable model that might reconstruct that decision across cases.
5. **Evidence and authority:** what witnesses the original rationale versus what is inferred later, and who can confirm which claim.

An actor's description of tacit or intuitive judgment is material **first-person evidence of their reported reasoning**, not automatic proof of a universal decision mechanism. Conversely, absence of prior formalization does not mean the reasoning was arbitrary, nonexistent or entirely recoverable.

## Investigation direction

The first objective is **to identify explanatory gaps**, not to construct a mandatory reasoning taxonomy, universal optimization function, new VSlices product or universal protocol.

For each bounded real case where a procedure or decision exists but the reasoning that led to it is unclear:

1. **Choose an actual decision** and its context; prefer preserved contemporaneous evidence where available.
2. **Reconstruct the available alternatives and pressures** using records and the actor's account; label contemporaneous observations separately from later recollection and interpretation.
3. **Ask what distinction made the selected course defensible** and what different evidence would have led to another course. Do not translate every answer into existing Paths merely because the vocabulary exists.
4. **Contrast against a rival explanation or counterexample**; test whether the proposed distinction predicts or discriminates a decision not used to formulate it.
5. **Classify the gap by responsibility**: a Method selection/judgment, a Design methodology, a Protocol execution procedure, a specialized product semantics problem, an incomplete evidence trail, or a genuinely shared mechanism question. Do not decide ownership by where a candidate note was written.
6. **Keep the smallest supported outcome**: an observation, a documented rationale, a revised existing mechanism, a new candidate or an explicit unknown. Mechanism creation and promotion require additional evidence.

A useful provisional reconstruction skeleton is:

```text
case / decision:
contemporaneous context and evidence:
alternatives genuinely available:
reported judgment at the time (if recoverable):
later account or interpretation (if any):
explanatory gap in existing VSlices concepts:
candidate distinction and rival explanation:
what would change/refute that distinction:
owner / authority / next test:
```

This is a **thinking aid**, not a new mandatory file schema, checklist or recording obligation for every iteration. In particular, introspection alone may fail to recover the actual causal decision process.

## Known entry and open questions

**Initial witness:** selection of a Design modality. The Method concept [Modality Selection](../products/method/modality-selection.md) specifies current selection guidance, while [Research Input #23](https://github.com/vslices/research-space/issues/23) asks what actually explains the originating judgment. The first does not itself validate the second.

Further unexplained decisions should be **discovered**, not invented here as a catalog. Relevant questions:

- Which other VSlices procedures were stabilized before their originating selection criteria became explicit?
- Which gaps are missing decision evidence, and which require an additional semantic distinction?
- How do we avoid rationalizing decisions after observing the result?
- Are some important decisions situational or embodied enough that a single stable generalized mechanism would misrepresent them?
- What is the minimum representation that helps another actor understand or responsibly reproduce the judgment without replacing that actor's reasoning with a rigid algorithm?
- If mechanisms recur, are they distinct, reducible or merely context-dependent manifestations of other concepts?

## Ownership and next boundary

This candidate is recorded alongside Suite mechanisms **as an investigation of their possible explanatory insufficiency**, not as an assertion that Suite owns a new universal `Reasoning` mechanism.

- [Method](../products/method/README.md) owns its actual criteria for choosing/navigating VSlices product responsibilities.
- [Design](../products/design/README.md) owns its methodology meanings and constraints.
- [Protocols](../products/protocols/README.md) owns reusable executable procedures; their existence is not evidence that the decision mechanisms that led to them are known.
- [Research](https://github.com/vslices/research-space) may test explanatory hypotheses, rival models and counterexamples without being forced to validate the original intuition.

The intended result is **better fidelity between real reasoning and VSlices representations**, even if the review concludes that a particular judgment should remain situated, partly tacit, or undecidable from the available evidence.

**No universal mechanism, additional Path, decision algebra, optimization score or required documentation process is promoted by this note.**
